About

Qti π ports the cardiac ML line and app architecture from Health-Companion and adds a new ear-EEG neuro vertical inspired by Neuroear.

Models are trained on real, cited public datasets (see ml/DATASETS.md). This app is a screening aid, not a diagnostic device — always consult a medical professional.
